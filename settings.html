How to Authorize

  1. Ensure your Client Secret is entered above.
  2. Click Authorize. A new tab will open.
  3. Log in and select your workspace/pages.
  4. After you are redirected to "https://exply.", look at the address bar.
  5. Copy everything after code= and paste it into the field above.
  6. Click Exchange Code to save your permanent access token.
